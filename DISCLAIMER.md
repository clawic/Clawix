# Clawix Disclaimer

Last updated: 2026-05-18

## English

Clawix outputs can be incomplete, wrong, stale, biased, misclassified, or
unsafe. Review important outputs before relying on them.

Clawix does not provide medical, mental health, legal, financial, banking,
insurance, employment, education, government, emergency, security, safety, or
other regulated professional advice. Sensitive outputs are drafts for review,
not final decisions.

In an emergency, contact local emergency services or trusted local resources.
Clawix is not an emergency service and must not be used for urgent crisis
handling, self-harm intervention, violence response, medical emergencies, or
physical safety control.
