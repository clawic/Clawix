# Clawix Safety Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18

Clawix consumes the ClawJS regulated-domain safety boundary. Sensitive domains
may be visible with guards, but decisions remain blocked or review-gated.

## English

Allowed sensitive use: local recordkeeping, search, extraction, factual
summary, questions to review, gaps/provenance, non-final drafts, and
preparation for human or professional review.

Blocked sensitive use: final regulated decisions, diagnosis or treatment,
therapy or crisis counseling, final legal/financial advice, credit, insurance,
employment, education, public benefits, emergency response, physical safety, or
autonomous regulated filings.

Required review: external sensitive actions, export/share, remote/sync,
provider use, support diagnostics, publication, signing, payment, purchase,
filing, or submission. Some actions remain blocked even with approval.

Sensitive outputs must preserve labels equivalent to draft, not professional
advice, human review required, sources and gaps required, regulated domain, and
decision effect.

## Espanol

Uso sensible permitido: registro local, busqueda, extraccion, resumen factual,
dudas para revisar, gaps/procedencia, borradores no finales y preparacion para
revision humana o profesional.

Uso sensible bloqueado: decisiones reguladas finales, diagnostico o
tratamiento, terapia o apoyo de crisis, consejo legal/financiero final,
credito, seguros, empleo, educacion, ayudas publicas, emergencias, seguridad
fisica o filings regulados autonomos.

Revision requerida: acciones externas sensibles, export/share, remoto/sync,
proveedores, diagnosticos de soporte, publicacion, firma, pago, compra, filing
o submission. Algunas acciones siguen bloqueadas incluso con aprobacion.

Los outputs sensibles deben conservar etiquetas equivalentes a borrador, no es
asesoramiento profesional, revision humana requerida, fuentes y gaps
requeridos, dominio regulado y efecto de decision.
