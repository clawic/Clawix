Decisions board

The running ledger of the visual language. Decided calls are settled and being folded into the tokens and components. Experiments are in flight, still resolved through a discordance. To add are app components that exist but are not yet documented in the fundamentals. Nothing here is removed by force, only superseded after a confirmed comparison.

Toggle

Segmented

Low-luminance dark fill with a visible hairline; radius leaning to 10 (see discordance).
Menu surface
Subtle glass: light blur, opaque-ish tint, radius 12; selection must read clearly.

Glass squircle, radius 18; wins over the flat card.
