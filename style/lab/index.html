Style Lab
Where the visual language gets iterated, in isolation, before any change touches product code. The rule of the lab: never remove or override a curated treatment to force a fundamental. Drift is made visible, compared against a real reference, and only then decided.
1
Find the drift, don't fix it silently. When a surface breaks a rule or has quietly acquired a different radius, curve, weight or color, record it as a discordance instead of editing it away.
2
Show both, side by side, with the exact difference. Every discordance renders A and B live, describes precisely what differs, and asks one specific question. The decision is made on a validated visual, not on abstract rule text.
3
Record the verdict, then fold it into canon. Settled calls land on the decisions board and are then pushed into tokens.css / ui.css so the standard and every screen update together.
