# Clawix Regulated Domains

Last updated: 2026-05-18

Clawix follows the ClawJS executable regulated-domain policy. The official app
is 18+ by default and sensitive domains are visible only with guards,
disclaimers, labels, and review gates.

Covered domains include health, mental health, reproductive/intimate data,
pharma, finance, banking, insurance, legal, HR/employment, education,
government/public services, housing/real estate, compliance/GRC, identity,
security, billing/payments, labs/research, IoT/physical actions,
vehicles/transport, and minors.

## English

For each domain, Clawix may support local records, search, extraction, factual
summary, questions, gaps/provenance, non-final drafts, and preparation for
human or professional review. Clawix must not take final regulated decisions.

## Espanol

Para cada dominio, Clawix puede soportar registros locales, busqueda,
extraccion, resumen factual, dudas, gaps/procedencia, borradores no finales y
preparacion para revision humana o profesional. Clawix no debe tomar decisiones
reguladas finales.
