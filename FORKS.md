# Forks and compatible Clawix builds

Clawix is open-source software. You may fork, modify, redistribute, sell, or
build compatible products under the repository license. This guide explains how
to avoid confusing users about official app status.

## You may

- fork, modify, redistribute, sell, or host your own version under the
  repository license;
- say that your project is a fork of Clawix when that is true;
- say that your project is compatible with Claw or Clawix when the statement is
  truthful and scoped;
- build from source for yourself, your organization, or your customers.

## You must avoid confusion

Use a distinct app name, bundle identity, icon, website, package, service, and
support channel for modified or third-party distributions. Do not imply that
your build, package, registry, service, support channel, website, or update
feed is official unless upstream maintains or authorizes it.

Use compatibility language instead of official language:

- Good: "compatible with Claw"
- Good: "a fork of Clawix"
- Not OK without permission: "Official Clawix"
- Not OK without permission: "Clawix official build"
